# Code of Conduct

## Our Promise

Zen Notebook is a project built around a simple belief:

**Less noise. More thought.**

We want to build a community where people can share ideas, improve software, and collaborate without unnecessary hostility or drama.

Everyone who participates in this project is expected to help maintain a respectful and welcoming environment.

## Expected Behavior

We ask all contributors to:

* Treat everyone with respect, regardless of experience or background.
* Give constructive feedback instead of personal attacks.
* Welcome beginners and help them learn.
* Respect different opinions and technical approaches.
* Focus discussions on improving Zen Notebook.
* Accept reasonable criticism and explain disagreements respectfully.
* Give credit to other people's work.
* Respect the time and effort of contributors.

You do not need to be an expert to contribute. Thoughtful questions, bug reports, documentation, testing, and code improvements all matter.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

* Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
* Discrimination or hateful language.
* Deliberate intimidation or humiliation.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Trolling or repeatedly disrupting discussions.
* Abusing contributors because of their skill level, opinions, or mistakes.
* Any other conduct that makes participation hostile or unsafe.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Disrespect is not.

## How We Handle Disagreements

Not every contributor will agree on every decision.

Explain your reasoning, listen to alternatives, and focus on the project's goals.

Maintainers may reject changes that do not fit Zen Notebook's purpose, introduce unnecessary complexity, or fail to meet quality requirements. Decisions should be communicated respectfully.

## Reporting a Problem

For general project questions and technical issues, please use GitHub Discussions or Issues.

If a concern requires privacy, do not post personal information publicly. Contact the project maintainer through an available private communication method.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and respectfully.


## Consequences

Depending on the situation, maintainers may issue a warning, remove inappropriate content, temporarily restrict participation, or exclude someone from the project.

Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents or participates in Zen Notebook.

## Our Principle

We are here to build useful software, learn from each other, and respect the people behind the code.

**Challenge ideas. Improve the code. Respect the people.**

— Zen Notebook Project
