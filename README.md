# Zen Notebook

### Less noise. More thought.

**A notebook for people who want to write—not be interrupted.**

Windows has been adding AI experiences to everyday computing. Traditional tools are also changing, becoming more complex than some users need.

Zen Notebook takes a different direction.

No forced AI. No unnecessary distractions. Just a notebook built around the simple act of writing.

---

## The Problem

### Not everything needs Copilot.

Sometimes, you open a notebook because you have an idea to capture, a thought to organize, or something important to remember.

You don't necessarily need an AI assistant beside your text. You don't need another feature competing for your attention. And you shouldn't have to navigate extra complexity just to write a sentence.

**Your thoughts should be the main feature—not the software surrounding them.**

### Notepad forgot what a notebook was supposed to be.

A notebook used to be a place where you could open a blank page and write.

Now, even a simple text editor can feel like it's trying to become something else. More features, more integrations, more things to learn.

Not every user wants a Swiss Army knife when they asked for a pencil.

Zen Notebook is built on a simple belief: **a tool can be powerful by knowing what not to include.**

---

## Our Philosophy

### 1. Simplicity over feature overload

A notebook should make writing straightforward. Every feature should have a reason to exist.

### 2. You stay in control

Your ideas belong to you. Zen Notebook is designed around your writing experience, not around pushing an AI assistant into it.

### 3. Less noise. More thought.

Your attention is valuable. The interface should support your thinking instead of competing with it.

### 4. Useful features, not feature collecting

Creating, finding, editing, organizing, and managing notes should feel natural. Features should solve real problems—not exist merely to make a feature list longer.

---

## What Is Zen Notebook?

Zen Notebook is a Java-based notebook project with a lightweight web interface.

Its goal is to offer a focused writing environment with:

* Create and manage notes
* Search for saved notes
* Edit existing notes
* Favorite important notes
* Move unwanted notes to the trash and restore them
* A simple browser-based interface
* Local database storage using H2
* A Windows desktop-style launch experience

Features may evolve as the project develops.

---

## Why Zen Notebook?

| The usual direction        | The Zen Notebook direction   |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Add more because you can   | Add only what helps          |
| Put more things on screen  | Keep attention on the task   |
| Make AI part of everything | Let users choose their tools |
| Compete for attention      | Respect attention            |
| Complexity as progress     | Simplicity with purpose      |

Zen Notebook isn't trying to win a feature-count competition.

It's trying to make opening a notebook feel simple again.
